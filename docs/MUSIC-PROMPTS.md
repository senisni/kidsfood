# Промпты для музыки

Для Suno, Udio или похожего генератора. Все три — без вокала, около 70 секунд, 100 BPM, мажор. Темп 100 BPM спокойнее прежних 120: успеваешь прочитать. Чистое окончание без затухания — так проще подогнать финал.

Структура, общая для всех: вступление 4 такта → тема → короткая остановка на 1 такт (вопрос «Кого не хватает?») → тема шире → средняя часть мягче (сад в животе) → вечерняя, тише → финальный акцент.

Когда сгенерируете, положите файл в `video/music/`. Я размечу удары и подгоню монтаж под трек.

## 1. Бумажный марш

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals. Playful whimsical children's animation score, 100 BPM, F major, ~70 seconds. Pizzicato strings, glockenspiel, celesta, light hand claps, woodblock, soft upright bass, small snare brushes. Wes Anderson storybook feel, paper cut-out animation, curious and warm, not silly. Structure: 4-bar intro with plucked strings; bouncy main theme on glockenspiel; one full bar of complete silence (a dramatic pause) around 0:13; theme returns fuller with claps; softer dreamy middle section with celesta and harp around 0:40–0:50; calm gentle evening section; clean final hit ending, no fade-out.
```

## 2. Укулеле и свист

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals. Sunny acoustic folk-pop for a children's educational cartoon, 100 BPM, C major, ~70 seconds. Ukulele strumming, whistled melody, acoustic guitar, hand claps, shaker, toy piano accents, warm upright bass. Friendly, optimistic, Scandinavian kids TV vibe. Structure: 4-bar ukulele intro; whistled main melody; one bar of total silence (stop-time pause) around 0:13; full band returns with claps; gentle music-box middle section around 0:40–0:50; soft lullaby-like evening part; short cheerful ending chord, no fade-out.
```

## 3. Маримба и калимба

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals. Gentle playful children's animation music, 100 BPM, G major, ~70 seconds. Marimba, kalimba, vibraphone, soft felt piano, light percussion (shakers, rim clicks, bongos), round warm bass. Tactile, handmade, curious, like a picture book coming to life. Structure: 4-bar marimba intro; light bouncy theme; one bar of complete silence around 0:13 as a question pause; theme returns brighter; soft magical kalimba section around 0:40–0:50; calm warm evening section with felt piano; clear final note ending, no fade-out.
```

## Если генератор не держит паузу

Не страшно: паузу вырежу в монтаже. Важнее темп около 100 BPM и чистое окончание.
